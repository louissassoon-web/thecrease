# Recorded sound shortlist

The shipped R37 effects are generated audio. No recording below has been downloaded, embedded or claimed as part of this release. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403 in this environment, so the game retains an offline, dependency-free palette with lower levels, several impact variants and scattered claps instead of pitched crowd drones. This does not establish naturalness on a phone speaker.

Verified primary source pages on 4 November 2026:

- Cricket ball bouncing on a bat.wav, GH0STY_XD: https://freesound.org/people/GH0STY_XD/sounds/594342/ . The author describes a real willow bat and cricket ball, recorded with a Zoom H4n and shotgun microphone. The page identifies CC0.
- Cricket (Sport), OroborosNZ: https://freesound.org/people/OroborosNZ/sounds/141250/ . A recording in the crowd at a T20 match. The page identifies CC0 and requests a courtesy credit. Before inclusion, listen for announcements/music, cut appropriate applause-only sections, level-match and credit the author.
- Freesound licence guidance: https://freesound.org/help/faq/ . Use CC0 or suitable CC-BY assets, retain source and licence evidence, and avoid non-commercial recordings for a monetised game.
- ZapSplat is another option for games: https://www.zapsplat.com/can-i-use-your-sound-effects-in-my-project/ . Its account/licence and attribution terms require review before selection; no purchase or account has been made.

For production, recorded close bat contacts, gloves, turf and separate crowd reactions should replace the corresponding SoundPalette samples. Trim and embed the licensed clips in the HTML, pre-decode on user interaction, retain mute/volume settings and avoid runtime network requests. No game-engine redevelopment is needed.

Vibration references: https://www.w3.org/TR/vibration/ and https://w3c.github.io/vibration/reports/implementation.html . Chrome requires prior activation and restricts cross-origin iframes. A true return value does not verify a motor. Google Pixel settings reference: https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/2819577?hl=en-GB .
