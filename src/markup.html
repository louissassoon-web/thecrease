Share game

Anyone with the testing link can play. Each browser has its own matches and saves.

When a shot looks wrong, copy the ball details and send them with what you expected. A screen recording helps with movement or graphics.

Build 35. Your save is not included in the game link or ball details.

How to play

Bat
Watch the ball. Swipe towards your shot. Lift when it reaches the bat.
Hit high
Pull back, then swipe forwards. A short swipe defends. No swipe leaves the ball.
Bowl
Swipe sideways to aim. Pick a ball. Hold, then flick up as the arm comes over. Earlier is fuller. Later is shorter.
Run
The first safe run starts for you. Tap Run 2 or Run 3. Three is the limit. Tap Hold to cancel a run that has not started.
Field
Tap a ringed fielder for a catch. For a run-out, tap a wicket. Play keeps moving. The field throws for you if you wait.
Take a break
Tap Menu to pause. Your game saves itself. Settings has sound, vibration and the screen view.
More cricket tips

Drive full balls. Cut or pull short balls. Sweep full spin. Watch the shadow to judge the bounce.

T20 has 20 overs each. Tests have two innings each and five days. You can declare between balls from the pause menu.

Choose a bowler before each over. Simulate over lets the computer finish it. Fielders change position before the next ball.

Fast-forward speeds up a chase. A run-out chance returns to normal speed. Tap Fast-forward again after the throw.

The shop opens between matches. Win matches to earn coins for kits and grounds.

If your swings are always early or late, try Timing offset in Settings.
